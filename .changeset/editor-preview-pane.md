Adds a preview beside the content editor. For collections with previews, the editor bar has a **Show the site beside the editor** button that opens the entry's draft next to the fields, at desktop or phone width. The preview reloads after each save, and the panel stays open across entries until it is closed. It is shown on screens at least 1024 pixels wide.

On a multi-site admin host, **Preview**, **Live View**, and the new panel now open the page on the site's own domain instead of the admin host's.

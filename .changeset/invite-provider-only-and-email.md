Makes invitations easier to accept and send on a shared multi-site admin.

- On an admin that signs in only through providers (`multiSite.signInDomains`), the invitation page offers only the provider buttons, with no passkey setup.
- When no email provider is configured, the "Invitation created" dialog has an **Email invite** button. It opens the person's email app with the invitation written and the link included, ready to send.
